about me

KHOA BUI


I was born and raised in Vietnam. I moved to the U.S. about ten years ago, and this October will be my tenth anniversary. I transferred to San Francisco State University from Contra Costa College. My major is Visual Communication Design. I am taking this class as a prerequisite, and I also think this is an important part of a graphic designer’s job, so I want to gain more knowledge and experience in this field.

I graduated from a university in my country with a B.A. in Accounting and Auditing. I worked as an accountant for 1.5 years and then moved here. I’m not very good at drawing and don’t have much of a background in design. However, I like taking pictures and editing them, and I’m interested in exploring the field of design, so instead of pursuing my former major, I decided to change to Visual Communication Design. Hopefully, this is the right major for me.
