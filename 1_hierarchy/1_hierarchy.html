Beleriand

Lay of Leithian, Canto II, vv. 399-400


The region of north-western Middle-earth during the First Age.


Geography

In the First Age, Beleriand was among the westernmost lands of Middle-earth. Beleriand had a long western shoreline with the Great Sea Belegaer, including the Bay of Balar in its south-west.The Ered Luin (Blue Mountains) formed the eastern border of Beleriand, separating it from Eriador. To the south, Beleriand "had no sure boundaries; for there were pathless forests in those days between the unpeopled shores and the lower waters of [the river] Gelion. Beleriand stretched about 550 miles broad from Eglarest on its western shore to the Blue Mountains in the east.


Etymology

Beleriand translates to "The Country of Balar" with the ending -ian(d) for places and countries. The OS form could have been *Balariandë (a slid to e because of the following i).

The element Balar refers to Ossë, who often dwelt at the shores (although this refers to the earlier legendarium when Ossë and Uinen were Valar of the sea, not Maiar of Ulmo).
