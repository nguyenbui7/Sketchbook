Here are some artists who inspire me

Lady Gaga

Lady Gaga is not only a singer, songwriter, and actress but also a creative artist. Her creativity can be seen in her outfits, dance choreography, album covers, and the stories she wants to tell through each song and performance. She inspires me with how much attention to detail she puts into her work from the visuals to the music itself.

Her performance of Paparazzi at the 2009 VMAs was one of her most iconic live performances. It was an amazing performance that also inspired me to appreciate creative art.



Gabi Wolf

Gabi Wolf is an illustrator and graphic designer based in Berlin. She creates many coloring books for adults and has a “cozy art” style. I follow her on Instagram where she shows how colors are used and combined to create a three-dimensional feeling in her drawings. Her use of color inspires me to explore how different colors can be mixed and matched to create visual effects.



Mitchell Toy

Mitchell Toy is an author and illustrator based in Melbourne. I also follow him on Instagram. While Gabi Wolf mostly draws objects and cute things, Toy creates comic books with drawings that convey depth and movement. I can understand the story he is telling even before reading any of the text. I’m inspired by the sense of movement he creates in his illustrations.
