11/09/26 I really enjoyed working on this assignment because whenever I viewed my website on my iPhone or iPad, the layout always looked messy and different from how it appeared on my MacBook. I know the main focus of this assignment is not “Does this work on an iPhone?”, but it actually helped me solve some of the problems I had when viewing my website on different screen sizes.

I chose two breakpoints: 1200px and 600px. The 1200px breakpoint is mainly for tablets, such as the iPad mini, 11-inch iPad, and 13-inch iPad, while the 600px breakpoint is for smaller screens like the iPhone.

This is how my home page looks at screen widths between 601px and 1200px.

At this point, I made the navigation text smaller and the astronaut slightly larger. I also reduced the astronaut’s movement since the screen was getting smaller. Besides that, I adjusted the font sizes of my introduction text and made sure the text below was aligned with the text above.

And this is how my home page looks at 600px.



At 600px, I literally had to redesign the entire layout to make everything fit properly. Unlike the 1200px breakpoint, where I only needed to adjust a few things, the 600px breakpoint required a lot more changes. First, I changed the navigation bar from a horizontal layout to a vertical layout because there wasn’t enough space for all the links to fit in one row. I also had to adjust the font sizes, margins, and padding of my introduction text to make everything fit on the smaller screen.

Another thing I had to consider was the astronaut animation. Since the screen was much smaller, I needed to adjust its size and movement effect. I think the 600px breakpoint was the most challenging part of this assignment because I couldn’t just make everything smaller. I had to think about how to rearrange the elements while still keeping the overall design of my website.
